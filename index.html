What it is

› Blocks, not scrollback

Every command and its output is one addressable thing you can filter, search, copy and re-run.

› An editor that came with it

Open a file, search a codebase, read a diff — without leaving the terminal or waiting on a language server you had to install.

› Remote work over your own SSH

Browse, edit and run on any host you can reach. One small daemon travels over the connection you already have.

› Agents that survive a disconnect

Claude Code, Codex and friends keep running when the link drops, and pick up the same conversation when you come back.

› Your keys, your models

AI runs against credentials you supply and endpoints you choose, including ones on your own hardware.

› Nothing phones home

No account to make, no telemetry to opt out of, and no server round-trip between pressing enter and getting a shell.
