Feedback

Report a problem, ask for something Kyon does not do yet, or tell us what works for you.

Where to say it

Feedback goes to the issues of Kyon's public repository on GitHub, where you can also see what others have reported and follow what happens to it.

  • Look through the issues first: what you found may be known already, and adding to it helps more than a second report.
  • Open a new issue for a problem, or for something you would like Kyon to do.

Inside Kyon, Feedback in the Kyon menu, or in the menu behind the button at the right of the title bar, brings you here.

Reporting a problem

A report someone can act on says what happened, what you expected instead, and how to make it happen again. Three things make it much easier to find:

  1. The version. Open About Kyon and press the copy button beside the version.
  2. What you were connected to. This machine or a server, and for a server whether you signed in from a terminal or Kyon connects itself, as Working on a server describes.
  3. The log. Run View Kyon logs and attach kyon.log, or the lines around the time it happened. It says what Kyon did with your terminals, agents and connections. Read it before you share it: it names the folders you worked in and can quote the last thing a terminal you signed in from printed.

A screenshot helps for anything you can see: something drawn wrongly, cut off or in the wrong place.

Asking for something new

Say what you were trying to do and where Kyon got in the way, rather than only the feature you have in mind: there is often more than one way to give you what you need, and the problem is what makes the case for it.
